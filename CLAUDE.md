# Niches

A phone-first social app (web second) for Gen Z built around **niche feeds the user toggles between one at a time** — no mixed home feed. Pitch: **"You pick the niche. The feed ends. Likes are private."** Focus on connection, self-improvement and inspiration; no attention-bait.

The founder has little coding experience and wants to be involved: explain decisions in plain language and ask for their opinion on product and design choices before committing to them.

## Decisions so far

- **First version is a working demo** to show and test the idea.
- **Launch niche: Cooking.** **Art and Photography are open for posting** (founder, Nov 9 2026); Gym is still "coming soon". **Gym stays** (decided Nov 2026); goals track performance and habits (not weight), stats are hideable, and before/after photos sit behind a tap. Gardening is the leading candidate for the next niche, then Knitting & Crochet.
- **Feeds:** each niche feed shows *every* post in that niche, newest first, and ends (no more loads; the "You're caught up" card was removed Nov 2026, the founder finds it implied). A "Following" filter comes later. One post belongs to exactly one niche. Remember the user's last niche.
- **Feed layout:** both a single-column Posts layout and a Pinterest-style Grid, with a Posts/Grid switch on every niche (hidden only while a niche's Shop is showing). Tapping a grid tile opens the normal post view (cheers, comments) with a back button to the grid.
- **Each niche has its own color — a must.** Cooking is basil green (#1E6B47); Art ultramarine; Photography amber; Gym raspberry. Switching niche re-tints the app. Mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/7T9p36YdKk7gkBDfeQe6sm (founder approved the look; expect more feedback on real prototypes).
- **Niche marker outside Home:** on Discover and Saved (anywhere niches mix), each post gets a **border in its niche's color — border only, no label**. Keep the two tabs consistent.
- **Discover** = find people to follow + "inspiration this week" across niches the user filters for, ordered by most saved in the last 7 days. Never show the counts. Home stays newest-first.
  **Redesign built (founder, Nov 10 2026; canvas page "Discover · together"):** "Discover" + "Week of …" over a thin rule, the white pill search bar (no camera/photo search for now), "Explore by niche" collage cards (tap one → that niche's page, flooded in its color, with topic tabs and its "This week's favorites" Pinterest grid ending "That's this week's Cooking favorites.", people to follow, and a link to its feed), then This week's favorites, Creators to know, From the shops, ending "That's this week's favorites." Search is Instagram-style: suggestions with niche tags, people and recent searches while typing; results in tabs (Top · People · Posts · Shop · Plans) with related searches. **No pill chips:** filters are "quiet tabs" (plain words, line under the selected one, niche-colored for niches; founder picked option A over a serif index).
- **Sheets/menus don't dim the screen behind them** (founder feedback) `Sheet` lifts above the keyboard (`KeyboardAvoidingView`), so text boxes in sheets stay visible while typing.
- **Swipe left/right on Home switches niche like turning a page:** the next niche's feed slides in alongside the current one under your finger; header color blends between niches as you drag. Niche filters on Saved (and Plans) are quiet tabs; the selected niche is underlined in its color.
  Order: Cooking → Art → Photography → Gym. Tabs stay tap-only.
- **Demo has posts** from made-up founding creators in Art and Photography; the Post tab lets you pick Cooking, Art or Photography (it follows the niche you're in). Gym has none.
- **Post "⋯" menu, like Instagram:** own posts — edit, adjust grid preview, add to/remove from your Top 4 (any niche; if full, opens the picker to swap), hide cheer count, turn off comments, archive, delete; others' posts — save, not interested, report, copy link.
- **Look: "Soft Flood" (founder's pick, Nov 2026, plain background, no dots).** Canvas with all options: https://claude.ai/artifact/WcSK5feoqtRFASzgGTprDw. A dusty mid-tone of the niche color (`niche.mid`, accent mixed 34% with white) fills screens that belong to one niche (Home, a post, a goal); text there is the niche ink, never the accent. **Everything else (Profile, Discover, Saved, Post, Plans, forms) sits on one app-wide background, Cream by default** (`APP_GROUNDS`/`groundSurface()` in theme.ts, `useAppGround()`, chosen in the Color lab: Cream, Blush, Oat), so those screens look the same whichever niche was last open on Home (founder, Nov 2026). The tab bar is the niche's mid tone on Home and the app background elsewhere. Profiles never follow the last Home niche: the All tab shows Top 4 rows only for niches with picks (or your most active niche if none), and profile buttons, stats and the recap card are neutral (the app background's ink), never niche colors (founder, Nov 2026). Headlines are Instrument Serif (italic for niche names and moments like "You're caught up."), body is Schibsted Grotesk.
- **Goals confirmed (Nov 2026):** private by default like the diary; weekly streaks with rest weeks always available.
- **Niche colors are being explored:** a Color lab in Profile → Settings lets the founder try palettes per niche on device.
- **Profiles:** one profile per person with niche tabs (All · Cooking · Art ...).
- **Likes** (renamed from "cheers" in the UI; code still says `cheers`): counts hidden from everyone except the poster, who sees like and save counts on their own posts. No leaderboards.
- **Followers/following counts are shown on profiles, like Instagram** (founder's call, Nov 2026 — overrides the earlier research suggestion to defer them). Tapping a count opens the list of accounts.
- **Posts:** photos, text and video. Video is tap-to-play, no autoplay, no swipe-to-next feed, 60–90s cap.
- **Saved collections** (Pinterest-style; never call them "boards" in the UI) are a core feature, and can hold imported Instagram/TikTok saves: "Share to Niches" from the share sheet, plus one-time import of the user's Instagram/TikTok data export. No scraping, no re-hosting of their media.
- **Progress posts and goals** are a core feature.
- **Demo limits:** 18+, US only, no direct messages.
- **Users can request new niches** via feedback.
- **Notifications:** batched digest by default, real-time only for direct replies.

## Stack

Expo (React Native, Expo Router) for iOS, Android and web from one codebase; Supabase (Postgres, auth, storage, row-level security); Mux or Bunny Stream for video. TypeScript.

## Build order

1. Mockups of the main screens for founder feedback (including whether layout differs per niche: masonry grid vs. single-column posts)
2. App skeleton: sign-up (18+), bottom tabs (Home, Discover, Post, Saved, Profile), niche switcher
3. Posts, niche feeds with a real end, comments, private likes
4. Saved collections
5. Goals and progress posts
6. Safety basics: report, block, in-app account deletion, community guidelines (incl. AI-art and eating-disorder rules)
7. Share to Niches, Instagram/TikTok import, short video

## Roadmap the founder asked for (Nov 2026)

- **Phase A (demo polish):** post menu, Color lab, new Discover (people + inspiration), niche borders on Saved/Discover, swipe between niches.
- **Phase B, Plans:** one feature covering meal scheduling (shareable with friends), group meals where invitees pick a dish and claim who brings which ingredient, and photo/art outings (what + where + when). Each plan public or private.
- **Phase C, Shop ("Etsy meets Pinterest"):** users sell cookbooks, recipes, prints, artworks — **no food sales** (state cottage-food laws); listings attach to posts and are browsable per niche (Cooking, Photography, Art first). Demo checkout is pretend.
- **Phase D, Creators:** verified "celebrity" profiles and premium chefs/artists who charge a subscription or one-time fee for their best content. Same model for other niches. **Invite-only at first** (founder approves), open applications later. **Members-only posts appear in niche feeds and Discover only for people who can open them** (members, unlockers, the author); everyone else sees them locked on the creator's profile (`inFeeds()` in store). **Niches takes 5% of creator earnings** (founder confirmed Nov 2026; same as the Shop).
- **No forever scrolling, no engagement ranking in the main feed** (founder, Nov 2026, after weighing it): Home stays every post, newest first. Each niche's weekly **Favorites** (most saved this week, ends, new picks Mondays) lives on its Discover niche page, not on Home.
- **Future "For you"** (founder likes it, not started): an optional view, off by default, that learns from what *you* save and who you follow, and says why each post is there ("Because you saved 3 pasta posts").
- **More niches:** founder likes Gardening; wants other popular hobby suggestions.
- C and D need real accounts (Supabase) and payments; mind Apple/Google rules on paid digital content.
